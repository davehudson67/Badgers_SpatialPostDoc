WOODCHESTER V7b-M WITHIN-INDIVIDUAL SENSITIVITY

This is the V7b analogue of the V7a within-badger check.

It asks:
For badgers that acquire infection, are their infection events more likely
during risk periods preceded by their own high-mobility state than during
their own local-mobility risk periods?

It conditions out every stable individual characteristic.

Important:
- this is a sensitivity, not the primary hazard model;
- only event badgers contribute to conditional likelihood;
- the movement effect is mainly identified by event badgers whose movement
  exposure changes during their susceptible risk history;
- sex cannot be estimated because it is constant within badger.
